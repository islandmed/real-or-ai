How to Play Imposter

THE PREMISE

3 Real Photos · 1 Synthetic AI Imposter

Every day, a new case is released featuring 4 rounds of photographic evidence. In each round, 3 pictures were taken by real cameras in the physical world, and 1 picture was hallucinated by generative AI (Midjourney, Flux, Stable Diffusion). Your job is to spot and accuse the imposter!

FORENSIC TIPS

How to Spot AI-Generated Images

  • 🔍 Use the Zoom Button: Tap any photo or the Zoom button to inspect micro-textures at 2.2x magnification.
  • ✋ Anatomy & Fine Details: Check hands, fingernails, teeth, jewelry, and hair strands. AI often blends fingers or smooths skin unnaturally.
  • 💡 Lighting & Reflections: Check eye catchlights, glass reflections, and shadow angles. Real photos obey optical physics.
  • 🔤 Backgrounds & Text: AI frequently garbles background signs, leaves repetitive repeating textures, or turns far-off objects into melting geometry.
