Privacy

Imposter Game · play-imposter.lol

No account, no sign up. You never give us your name, email or any login. There is nothing to create and nothing to delete.

Your results stay on your device. Your score, streak and today's progress are saved in your browser's local storage so you can come back to them. They are not sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Your guesses. To check an answer, the game sends which photo you picked to our server. It is not linked to you and is not stored with any identifier.

Sharing. If you share a result, the link contains your case number, score and time so your friend can see it. Nothing else.

Hosting. The site is served by Cloudflare, which processes standard request data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Advertising. Imposter Game shows ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can turn off personalised advertising in Google's Ad Settings, and learn more in how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Analytics. We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies or track you across sites.

Photos. Real photos are credited on each case. AI images are made for the game.

Questions: message us on our Facebook Page.
